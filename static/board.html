Board

How it works

Drag a card to another column to change its status. You can drag any task you're assigned to, or any task if you're a team lead.

Every card also has a status dropdown, which works with a keyboard or on a phone.

Statuses that count as finished start folded. Click a folded column to open it; you can still drop cards on it.

Tasks waiting for a lead's approval can't be moved until they're approved.

Statuses for

These are the board's columns. To do, In progress and Completed are fixed; add your own, rename them, pick colours and reorder them. A status that counts as finished takes its tasks out of Overdue and Assigned to me.
